01 / ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
Driven to build.

I’m studying how we measure, understand, and improve the world through location-based data.

My interests sit where geomatics meets software and intelligent systems: using GIS, remote sensing, spatial analysis, and AI to turn complex information into useful tools. I’m especially interested in disaster resilience, safer mobility, and evidence-led decisions for Nepal’s challenging landscapes.

I enjoy learning by building — testing ideas, working through technical problems, and collaborating with people who want to make something meaningful.
